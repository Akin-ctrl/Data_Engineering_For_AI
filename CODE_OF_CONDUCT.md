# Code of Conduct

This is a learning space. We want everyone to feel welcome, whether you are in the bootcamp, a public learner, or a teacher.

## Expected Behavior

- Treat other people with respect.
- Assume people are trying their best, even if their question seems basic.
- Help others when you can.
- Ask questions. No question is too simple in a learning community.
- Give feedback that helps, not that hurts.
- Do not share anyone else's personal information without permission.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment of any kind.
- Discrimination based on identity.
- Bullying or repeated disrespect.
- Spam or deliberately disruptive messages.
- Anything that makes the space unsafe or unwelcoming.

## What To Do If Something Goes Wrong

If you witness or experience behavior that violates this code:

1. Tell the person, if you feel safe doing so.
2. Report it to the maintainer by email or by opening a private issue.

Reports will be taken seriously and handled confidentially.

Breaking these rules may result in:

- A warning
- Being removed from discussions
- Other actions as needed

## Scope

This code applies to GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other spaces where this community gathers.

Thank you for making this a respectful learning space for everyone.
